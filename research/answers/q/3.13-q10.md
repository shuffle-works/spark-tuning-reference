## Q10. What is resultSerializationTime in taskMetrics and under what workload profile does it dominate end-to-end task time?

`resultSerializationTime` is defined simply as "elapsed time spent serializing the task result," in milliseconds[^spark-history-server]. It sits next to `resultSize`, "the number of bytes this task transmitted back to the driver as the TaskResult"[^spark-history-server], so the amount of work `resultSerializationTime` measures scales with how much data a task ships back to the driver rather than with the size of its shuffle output. Beyond that, the retrieved corpus does not characterize a specific workload profile (e.g., `collect()`-heavy jobs, large accumulators, or actions returning large per-partition results) under which this metric dominates end-to-end task time.
