## Q8. What is the shuffle read overhead for distinct() vs dropDuplicates(cols) — do they differ in shuffle volume?

Both require a shuffle: Spark SQL's `dropDuplicates` selects unique rows the same way RDD-level `distinct` does ("this can require a shuffle"), and the source notes it's often much slower than a simple `filter`[^high-perf-spark]. The documented difference between the two is about key flexibility, not shuffle volume: unlike RDD `distinct`, DataFrame `dropDuplicates` can optionally drop rows based on only a subset of columns (e.g., `pandas.dropDuplicates(List("id"))`) rather than requiring uniqueness across the whole row[^high-perf-spark]. The retrieved material doesn't give a quantified, bytes-moved comparison between full-row `distinct()` and a column-subset `dropDuplicates(cols)`; answering that precisely would need a source on how the `Deduplicate`/`Aggregate` planner shapes the shuffle write for each case.
