## Q2. Why is ≤5 cores per executor the common recommendation — what is the connection to NUMA, OS jitter, and HDFS throughput?

The retrieved material grounds the ≤5-cores rule in HDFS client behavior, not in NUMA or OS-jitter effects: "HDFS client has trouble with tons of concurrent threads. It was observed that HDFS achieves full write throughput with ~5 tasks per executor. So it's good to keep the number of cores per executor below that number"[^spark-notes-executor-sizing]. The "fat executor" case (one executor per node, using all 16 cores) shows the failure mode: "with all 16 cores per executor... HDFS throughput will hurt and it'll result in excessive garbage [collection]"[^spark-notes-executor-sizing].
