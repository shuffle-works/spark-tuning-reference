## Q2. What is the exact per-row overhead of Pickle serialization in a Python UDF vs Arrow batch serialization in a pandas UDF? Are there published benchmarks?

The corpus has no published exact per-row (microsecond or nanosecond) overhead figure for pickle vs. Arrow serialization. What it does have is a relative, workload-level benchmark. Databricks' introductory blog post benchmarked three operations (Plus One, Cumulative Probability, Subtract Mean) on a 10M-row DataFrame with an Int and a Double column, on a single-node 6.0 GB / 0.88-core Databricks Community Edition cluster, and found that "Pandas UDFs perform much better than row-at-a-time UDFs across the board, ranging from 3x to over 100x"[^databricks-pandas-udf]. Learning Spark, 2nd Edition explains the gap qualitatively: pickled row-at-a-time UDFs require expensive JVM↔Python data movement, whereas Arrow-backed pandas UDFs skip serialization/pickling because the data already arrives in Arrow's columnar, Python-consumable format[^learning-spark-2e]. No source in the corpus decomposes this into a fixed per-row cost. [NEEDS-REVIEW: a precise per-row pickle-vs-Arrow cost breakdown would require a dedicated micro-benchmark paper or blog post not present in the indexed corpus.]
