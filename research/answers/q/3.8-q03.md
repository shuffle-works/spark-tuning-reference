## Q3. What are the pros and cons of df.write.format("noop").mode("overwrite").save() vs df.count() for forcing materialization of a cached DataFrame?

No source in the corpus discusses the `noop` write format, so a direct pros/cons comparison is [UNRESOURCED]. This would need Spark's DataFrameWriter/benchmark documentation on the `noop` data source, which isn't in the indexed reference set.

The corpus does cover the `count()` side. Caching is lazy, and the DataFrame "is not fully cached until you invoke an action that goes through every record"; `count()` is the usual action for forcing full materialization of the cache[^learning-spark-2e][^definitive-guide]. One caveat for any writer-vs-count comparison: on some plans `count()` can be satisfied via metadata/partial scans rather than reading every row, whereas a full data-writing action forces a genuine full pass. That claim about `count()`'s shortcut isn't established in this question's own retrieved chunks, so treat it as a general caution rather than a cited fact.
