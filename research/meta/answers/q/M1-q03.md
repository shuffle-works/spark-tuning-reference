## Q3. Why is the question, not the section, the atomic unit of the answering pipeline?

`docs/architecture.md` states the design directly: "the atomic unit of the answering pipeline is the **question**, not the section." It goes on to name `research/questions.yaml` as the single source of truth, holding `sections` (id, num, title, default_k) as a grouping label and `questions` (id, section, text, version, k, status) as the working records. Every other artifact, from per-question answer files up through section projections and content pages, is a derived projection regenerable from the registry and those answer files, never a source of truth itself.[^architecture]

That per-question state (its own `version`, `k`, `status`) is what makes independent recompute possible. `add_question.py` mints an id and appends a single `pending` record without mutating any existing record, and `recompute_question.py` flips exactly one record back to `pending` and backs up only that question's old answer file, touching no other record or file.[^rules-registry] If the section were the atomic unit, recomputing one question's answer would force treating the whole section's state as dirty; scoping status and versioning to the question lets one question be re-answered, re-versioned, or given its own `k` without disturbing its siblings.

The rest of the pipeline is built around that same grain: the Draft/Verify/Redraft agents are "each now scoped to a single question," and the `answer-question` skill runs one question end to end: Draft → Verify → [Redraft → re-Verify] → Humanize → `check_prose` → `project_section` → `content_state` ... → commit.[^architecture] The meta corpus's own `answer-meta-question` skill mirrors this: its input is one question id, and it dispatches the Draft/Verify agents with "a one-item question list = the record's text."[^skill-answer-meta-question] Only after a question is answered does `project_section` roll it into the section file, by concatenating that section's question answers in registry order and merging their `## Sources` blocks[^architecture]. The section projection is assembled from finished question-level work, not answered as a unit itself.
