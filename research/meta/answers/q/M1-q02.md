## Q2. What is the one-way derive-direction chain from research/questions.yaml down to index.html, and why does the architecture forbid editing a downstream artifact as a source of truth?

The chain runs in one direction, front to back:

`research/questions.yaml` → `research/answers/q/<id>.md` → `research/answers/<section-id>.md` → `content/*.md` (and `content/bottlenecks/*.md`) → `index.html`[^architecture].

Each stage has a role. `questions.yaml` is the registry of sections and questions with their statuses. Each per-question answer file holds that question's prose plus its own `[^id]` footnotes and `## Sources` block. The section file is a projection: an ordered concatenation of a section's question answers, with `## Sources` merged and deduped, and it is the same path the content manifest's `assembled_from:` already points at. Content pages are brief-driven and only first-generated from that projection, after which they become human-owned. The build turns the content pages into `index.html`[^architecture].

The architecture forbids treating any downstream artifact as a source of truth because `research/questions.yaml` is the single source of truth for the whole pipeline. Every other artifact (per-question answers, section projections, content pages) is a derived projection, regenerable from the registry and the per-question answer files, never edited as a source of truth itself[^architecture]. The registry rules state this directly: never edit a downstream artifact as if it were the source of truth, since per-question answers, section projections, and content pages are all derived projections regenerable from the registry, and recompute always starts at `questions.yaml` and flows forward[^rules-registry]. The derive-direction diagram states the same principle as an invariant: every arrow is one-way, and a downstream artifact (a section projection, a content page, the built site) is never a source of truth for an upstream one[^architecture]. The project's own `AGENTS.md` restates the practical consequence for operators: new or changed content still flows registry → answer → projection → page, and `questions.yaml` remains the authoritative question registry rather than any prose plan or derived array[^agents-md].
